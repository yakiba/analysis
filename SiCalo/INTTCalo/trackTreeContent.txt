trackTree content

0:  evt			event number
1:  track_id		ID of the track.
2:  x0			x of the silicon seed at the staring point
3:  y0			y of the silicon seed at the staring point
4:  z0			z of the silicon seed at the staring point
5:  px0			px of the silicon seed at (x0,y0,z0)
6:  py0			py of the silicon seed at (x0,y0,z0)
7:  pz0			pz of the silicon seed at (x0,y0,z0)
8:  eta0		eta of the silicon seed  at (x0,y0,z0)
9:  phi0		phi of the silicon seed at (x0,y0,z0)
10: pt0			pt of the silicon seed  at (x0,y0,z0)
11: chi2ndf		chi2/NDF of the silicon seed
12: dxy			2D DCA (sqrt(pow(xgvtx-t_x0, 2) + pow(ygvtx-t_y0, 2))
13: dz			z0 - zgvtx (or zgvtx - z0)?
14: charge		charge of this track (+1 or -1)
15: nmaps		# of Mvtx clusters of associated to this track?
16: nintt		# of INTT clusters of associated to this track?
17: innerintt		# of INTT hits at inner layer
18: outerintt		# of INTT hits at outer layer
19: crossing		beam crossing (int) = timebucket (=0 means triggered time)
20: x_proj_emc		x of the silicon seed track projected at EMC
21: y_proj_emc		y of the silicon seed track projected at EMC
22: z_proj_emc		z of the silicon seed track projected at EMC
23: px_proj_emc		px of the silicon seed track at EMC
24: py_proj_emc		py of the silicon seed track at EMC 
25: pz_proj_emc		pz of the silicon seed track at EMC 
26: eta_proj_emc	eta of the silicon seed track at EMC 
27: phi_proj_emc	phi of the silicon seed track at EMC 
28: pt_proj_emc		pt of the silicon seed track at EMC 
29: x_proj_oemc		x of the silicon seed track projected at EMC outer surface
30: y_proj_oemc		y of the silicon seed track projected at EMC outer surface
31: z_proj_oemc		z of the silicon seed track projected at EMC outer surface
32: x_rv_proj_emc	x of the silicon seed track w/ charged flipped projected at EMC
33: y_rv_proj_emc	y of the silicon seed track w/ charged flipped projected at EMC
34: z_rv_proj_emc	z of the silicon seed track w/ charged flipped projected at EMC
35: track_id_truth	MC truth track ID (ID of PHG4Particle). INTMAX for realdata
36: sicalo_pt		pt of Silicon-Emc track improved
37: sicalo_phi		same with track phi0,  phi of Silicon-Emc track at the origin (x0, y0, z0)
38: sicalo_eta		same with track eta0, eta of Silicon-Emc track at the origin (x0, y0, z0)
39: sicalo_dphi		phi difference between the closest EMC cluster and track projection
40: sicalo_dz		z difference between the closest EMC cluster and track projection
41: sicalo_emc_x        x of the associated Emc cluster
42: sicalo_emc_y	y of the associated Emc cluster
43: sicalo_emc_z	z of the associated Emc cluster
44: sicalo_emc_r	r of the associated Emc cluster
45: sicalo_emc_phi	phi of the associated Emc cluster
46: sicalo_emc_energy	energy of the associated Emc cluster
47: sicalo_emc_chi2	chi2 of the associated Emc cluster
48: sicalo_emc_prob	prob of the associated Emc cluster
